# Home ground concepts

Painted 2026-11-12 for approval. Base `act1` at `d7ee5aa`, branch `act1-region-home`.
These are concept references for Astra's later code-built models. They do not install terrain,
props, collision or game assets. Kausthubh has not approved this package yet.

The home should be recognizable from its land before any cottage is built. Two separate hills frame
a usable hollow: the Hall and pond to the north, the lone oak and shrine beside the eastern road.
The first green follows water and care. At hamlet, a green patch spreads beyond the fence while the
surrounding valley stays tired. Keep broad spaces between landmarks for player-built farms.

## Defaults, uncertainties and reading corrections

- The story's "48 blocks" does not say whether it means area or side length. I chose a 48 × 48 home
  boundary around the 32 × 24 settlement study. Today's `GRANT` is 28 × 28. Approval must settle the
  intended size before terrain or ownership code changes.
- The handbook says Hall 10 blocks away; the regional plan places its landmark 36 blocks north of
  the plot centre. I used a north-edge approach with a door at z -34, ten blocks past the proposed
  boundary. That reconciles the walking relationship, but is not an exact match to either existing dataset.
- The regional garden's retained world position is about 63 blocks west, whereas its drawn map point
  is 86 west. I followed the story's 60-block garden distance. The other coordinates above are proposed.
- The plan is a painted aerial diagram with tilted landmark symbols. It is not a survey or an
  orthographic construction drawing. The 60m arrow is drawn between enclosures; the intended distance
  is centre to centre. The pond appears farther north than its coordinate caption. Use the schedule
  for dimensions and placement, not pixel measurements or the painted scale bar.
- The lower section compares landmark heights. It is not a literal straight west-east transect;
  Hall and pond lie north of the field. The land and water levels need an implementation fit check.
- The vistas compress distance so both hills, garden and bridge fit the frame. The arrival camera
  also enlarges the shrine for readability. Keep its approved 5-block height in production.
- The arrival shed has warm weathered shingles, while the detailed road kit has teal planks. Use
  piece 19's teal plank roof. The first-night fire is the scene's focal light; production should keep
  the camp sheet's two-block clear separation from the tent.
- The bridge in the vistas reads as a shallow arch. Use the detailed kit's planked trestle structure,
  modest camber and dimensions. It still needs a walking surface supplied by the game.
- The day-one view has a few low shoots in its dry foreground. These are residual stubble, not a
  first harvest. No crop is ready when the hero arrives. Night adds the first watered strip.
- The hamlet's small wheel sits at the pond outflow to show water-led growth. Its working head,
  shafts and six farm rules need the parts builder's fit check. Keep one crop row in four resting,
  damp shaded mossling stones, room for the thistle run and a dark carrot area away from house lights.
  Do not place a lamp at every farm or cover all ground with machinery.
- The fox and residents are scale and story figures, not approved replacement character designs.
  The well grille and the meadow pond-rim module are proposals, not new quest mechanics.
- Tired and alive meadow pieces show repair as well as growth. The art does not decide whether
  repairs happen automatically or require the player's action.

## References and method

Read `docs/act1.md`, `docs/greenhollow-world.md`, `docs/painted-world.md`, the make-3d-asset skill,
`stone.md`, the settlement README, and handbook chapters 26, 22, 27 and 30. Compared the map and live
coordinate definitions where they differed. Viewed all five sacred sheets, the dead cottage, jetty,
settlement camp, homes and stages, approved spawn/farm/river screenshots, the six named map pieces,
and scenes 17-camp, 05-door, 04-fox, 07-morning, 21-hamlet and 28-runs-itself.

The built-in image generation tool painted each requested image. The tired vista was the edit
reference for alive and night to preserve the camera and land. Targeted edits removed an invented
second door and harvest from arrival, corrected the stile header and clarified the grey fox.
[prompts.json](prompts.json) records the prompts. Final WebPs use installed `cwebp` at quality 82,
1600 pixels wide. No repo tool, dependency install, game build or dev server was needed.

Verification applies to the concept files. The repo's verify-game skill was read; its runtime,
model and shipped-asset checks do not exercise source-only concept art. Each final WebP is opened
after compression and compared with the references above. File decoding, width, byte limits,
required-file presence and changed-path scope are checked separately before commit. No runtime
approval or model readiness is implied by completing these delivery checks.
