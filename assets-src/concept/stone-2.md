# Proposed ancient stone

The shared `stone.md` was absent on `world/concept-1` when these sheets were painted. This is a proposal for the coordinator to reconcile with that painter.

Use one pale honey limestone throughout the sacred and water landmarks. Base colour is approximately #D6C5A0, sunlit edges #F2E2BC, shaded faces #9A96A7. These are proposed material colours, not colours sampled from the images. The existing `public/assets/painted/rock.webp` supplies the broad cream and lavender brush language. The map pieces supply the warmer carved architecture.

Make large dressed courses with softened corners, shallow broad recesses and occasional chips. Keep joints narrow. Use painted variation rather than modelling each crack. Moss collects at feet and sheltered joints; it does not hide the silhouette. Carved leaves, spirals and arch panels should read at the game's camera distance.

Brass is warm, worn gold, approximately #B58B45, with small bright edges. Roof tiles are muted blue teal, approximately #397D7C. Tile colour is ordinary pigment. Emissive teal, approximately #68D9C5, means living magic and belongs in the shrine basin, Hall seam and waking waystone. Ordinary river water stays blue.
