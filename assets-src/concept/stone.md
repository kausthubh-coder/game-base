# Hearth limestone

Use this ancient stone across the High Road and terraces, and as the default for the other ancient landmarks. It is warm pale honey cream, with lavender-grey shade. The map's teal caps remain small accents. Gold belongs to active lume channels, not the whole stone surface.

- Base colour: #DEC398. Sunward faces: #F4DEB2. Shade: #9C91A7. These are proposed material colours before the game's shared light, not samples measured from a screenshot.
- Ashlar blocks are roughly 2 blocks long, 1 high and 1 deep. Use broad staggered seams and rounded bevels. Large structural masses carry the silhouette; most seams can be painted rather than separate geometry.
- Carving is shallow: a single leaf, paired leaf stems, or a straight recessed network channel. Avoid dense ornament. An extruded arch ring and a few wedges should carry an arch.
- Moss is sage #89936B, paler #A0A18E on the blighted terraces. It sits in damp joints, on upward ledges and at the foot. Leave most vertical faces bare cream.
- Roots are ochre brown #98613C with warm #C68B50 ridges and muted violet-brown shade. They lie over the masonry, bridge seams and pry a few blocks apart. They do not become stone carving. Use a few tapering swept tubes, 1.5 to 2 blocks thick for cart-sized roots.
- Muted teal caps: #648F88. Old brass: #B58C47. Active lume: #FFD266 with a small amber halo. Dormant lume and crystals: slate lavender #7E829E.

One block equals one metre. The hero is 1.8 blocks tall. Sheets show form and assembly; the world plan's dimensions and the corrections in report.md take priority over generated lettering or perspective proportions. Builders should fit their masses to those dimensions and retain each model's 6,000 triangle budget.
