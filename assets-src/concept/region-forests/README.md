# The forests

Concept package for approval, 2026-11-12. Painted with the built-in imagegen tool.
This is Astra's shape and material brief for the Old Forest and the northern grey forest.
Kausthubh's approval still precedes model work.

One block is one metre. The hero is 1.8 blocks tall. Dimensions below are width east-west,
depth north-south and height above the local ground, unless a piece states otherwise.
These dimensions take priority over perspective and the size of painted scale figures.

## Defaults and remaining interpretation

- Giant oak dimensions, the loop, grove D, clearing sizes, camp layout and den shape are proposals.
  Existing landmark sizes and map centres remain the build constraints.
- The anonymous Gardener statue and natural bee comb are visual defaults, not new lore.
- Keep full grey crowns, as the default grey look does today. Natural dead snags are scattered accents.
  Do not turn the whole grey forest into bare dead trees.
- The tree sheet's small plan icons for 05–07 look leafy. They mark the occupied area only.
  Build the bare forms in the three-quarter views. Plan-inset depth arrows are not height arrows.
- Scale figures are illustrative. In particular, the tree sheet exaggerates oak mass beside the hero;
  fit the numeric envelopes in the build list rather than measuring pixels.
- The ruins sheet isolates the stair on paper, making it look raised. Sink its top to ground and
  excavate to −3 as the approved forestStair section and corrected plan say. There is no doorway below.
- The plan's landmark icons are enlarged and shown obliquely for identification. Use its coordinates
  and listed footprints for placement. The scale bar refers to the grid, not the icons.
- The shrine icon in the plan is symbolic. Reuse the existing shrine model and its dormant/restored
  state; the yellow light drawn there does not specify a new light colour.
- The edge vista's lit road illustrates the cause of Blight. At this map's boar longitude the road
  is initially dark. Use the actual High Road lighting state when building the encounter.
- Bramble, Wren, the foxes and traveller here are scene markers. Their own approved creature and
  people sheets govern their final designs.

## Verification

All nine final WebPs were opened after compression and checked for silhouette, readable labels,
materials and story placement. Each is exactly 1600 pixels wide and below 600,000 bytes.
The kit numbers run from 01 to 28 without gaps. The corrected plan retains the fixed landmark
centres and removes the draft's invented masonry cathedral and open cave icon.

This package changes only unshipped concept files. No runtime code, manifest, dependency or
placement data changed. Per the painting-only brief, no repo tool, install, build or browser
playtest was needed. Game verification was scoped to the actual deliverables: opening every
final image, checking dimensions and byte limits, and inspecting the Git paths.
