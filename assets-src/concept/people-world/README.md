## Defaults and review limits

Chapter 28 and `playthrough/content.js` (`people`) govern story and equipment. Hair, skin, face and exact height choices are proposals where those sources are silent. White and gold identify the Order; Calla retains the required grey coat. Maren's teal is unlit cloth. Tiny generated half-sun details must become exactly six upper rays in production sprites. Generated numeric heights are not canonical and must not override the cast lineup or Dax's relative height.

Built-in `image_gen.imagegen` edited each original sheet with a character-specific identity brief. Shared direction and the character table describe the prompt set: preserve parchment composition, warm upper-left light, brown outlines, front/right-side/back, calm/glad/troubled portraits, corrected named palette and small pixel mock; apply the new identity consistently in every view. No new runtime assets or manifest entries. See [home cast](../people-home/README.md) for lineup, process and verification, and `../people-home/verification.json` for encoded-file checks. All concepts await approval.

Aldric's generated half-sun still includes extra lower rays. The six-upper-ray production rule above remains unresolved in the painting. Ren's generated footer incorrectly called him a Guild novice; that footer was cropped away, leaving his correct Order-white sash, two water skins and all required sheet panels.
