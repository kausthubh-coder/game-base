# Ancient stone for groups 5 and 6

The ruins use warm cream limestone with lavender-grey shade, broad worn chamfers and sparse moss in damp joints. Large masses and rounded arches carry the silhouette. Paint most masonry seams. Use cylinder segments for the tower and columns, boxes for walls and swept rings for arches. Keep roots separate from stone carving.

I checked the stone specification on `world/concept-1` while painting. Its Hearth limestone fits these sheets. Use its proposed material colours when the coordinator joins the groups: base #DEC398, lit faces #F4DEB2, shade #9C91A7, moss #89936B, roots #98613C with #C68B50 ridges, and old brass #B58C47. These are proposed material colours, before the game's lighting.

The Order has cleaner white plaster and ivory limestone, polished brass, amber glass and gilding. Its gold light stays steady. Hollowmere's chalky teal roof tiles are matte paint. Ancient ruins in this set have no active glow. Grey grass immediately borders the root pump's gold machinery.

The sheets describe shapes and assembly. Fit the models to the dimensions in `docs/greenhollow-world.md`, with a 1.8-block hero and a maximum 6,000 triangles per landmark. Read the build decisions in `report-3.md` before copying incidental painted details.
