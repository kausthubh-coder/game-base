Each sheet has front, side and back, a 1.8-block hero, attack panels, material palette and a small silhouette. Keepers are ancient maintenance machines. They shut down rather than receive the beasts' calming action.

## Defaults and cautions for approval

- Keeper dimensions, joint designs, rear cores and attack tells beyond the handbook's stated rules are proposals.
- The 1.8-block hero is a scale reference. Hero clothing and companion appearances defer to their own approved character sheets.
- The Sluice Keeper uses paddle-rim wheels so flooding has a clear visual cause. Its room channel is 6 blocks wide to fit its 4-block body.
- Lamplighter's large bottom SWITCH LAMPS panel is authoritative. Some unchanged introductory generated prose suggests the sentry delivers a push from its rear core. Disregard that wording. The rear core is the hero's strike target. The hero controls the kit lamp.
- Wild Harvester's companion brace is an action. A support drawn in the room is optional scenery, never an automatic substitute for the companion.
- The keeper turnarounds govern identity wherever a smaller room illustration simplifies the machine. Exact sprite pixel dimensions remain undecided.
