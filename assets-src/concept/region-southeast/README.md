# Lantern Marsh and the root caves

Approval concepts for act 1, painted 2026-11-12. This package proposes the terrain and dressing around the approved landmarks. It does not approve itself or change the game. Kausthubh reviews it before Astra builds the models.

One block is one metre. The hero is 1.8 blocks tall. All sizes below are width × depth × height. Height is the complete object's envelope unless a separate ground level is stated. These tables and the approved landmark dimensions take priority over painted perspective and generated lettering.

## Local layout defaults

The world map still puts Lantern Marsh below Hollowmere in the south-east and the root caves beyond the Mere in the north-east. The two panels are separate local schematics. Do not tile them beside each other. World scale remains the handbook's roughly 800 by 600 blocks.

The proposed marsh study covers 120 by 160 blocks. The river is roughly 24 wide locally. The 36 by 32 yard has the floodable keeper channel, bypass sluice and a dry lever route. Place the house on the eastern bank, with its wheel in the river on its western facade. Water goes south. The existing world plan decides the final bend and exact bank profile. The weir needs a bypass feeding the keeper channel so flooding it is a visible player action.

The cave study covers 64 by 80 blocks. The first chamber is 28 by 24 with twelve blocks of clear height. Shelves descend from level zero to minus six; a level bridge crosses there, then stairs beyond descend to a 16 by 12 landing at minus twelve before the Gallery door. Keep at least three blocks clear along the main route. Leave a clear outdoor court for the rootsnare and companion rescue. These concepts stop at the Gallery door. Its Lamplighter arena, switchable lamps and beam-blocking cover belong to the room package.

## Uncertainties and decisions

- Exact banks, local contours, cave depth and chamber sizes are not specified by the handbook. The values above are proposed defaults for approval, not changes to the world data.
- Distant buildings and waterfalls are atmosphere in the vistas. They are not extra cities, waterworks buildings or required placements. Keep the approved world-map landmarks and sightlines when building.
- Ordinary lanternflies are amber, with loose gatherings around light. Lanterns should follow the existing brightest-light rule; frogs keep their pond habitat. The paintings are an illustrative moment, not a measured simulation of attraction radii.
- Burrowers need soft scree margins and short burrow mouths; gearlings need uncluttered passages near old machinery. They are habitat requirements here. Creature designs belong to the creature package.
- Fishing equipment and eel traps are dressing. No fishing or eel-farm mechanic is approved by this sheet.
- The marsh shrine is dormant in both vistas. Its restored variant must reuse the approved teal basin treatment.
- Modules are visual kits. Terrain cuts, collision, bridge floors, water flow, moving mechanisms and light states need separate implementation. The exterior terrain must actually open over the cave mouth.
- Follow the existing prop budgets when modelling. Grand scale comes from the silhouette and repeated modules, not dense ornament. No geometry or runtime performance claim is made by these paintings.

## Sources and method

Read `docs/act1.md`, `docs/greenhollow-world.md`, `docs/painted-world.md`, the asset skill and `assets-src/concept/stone.md`. Visually compared the approved Pump House and weir, cave and Gallery mouths, crystals and ways-down vista, stilt hut, boardwalk, rock arch, marsh vista and shrine. Checked painted mud, reeds, scree and rock. Checked the handbook's whole map, Pump House and Gallery map pieces, and scenes 12, 13 and 27. Story references were chapters 04d, 23, 26 and 30, plus `playthrough/farms.js`.

Made with the built-in image-generation tool. The prompt set asked for soft gouache, honey limestone with lavender shade, old brass, sage vegetation, warm roots and a 1.8-block hero. Vista prompts specified the scenes described above and the approved landmark sizes. Kit prompts specified each numbered item and size verbatim from the tables. The plan prompt specified the two independent local layouts above. Corrections restored the basin shrine, fixed plan geography and removed misleading generated scale bars. Night uses the daylight vista as its reference and preserves its objects while changing light, mist and lanternfly activity.

No game code, shipped assets or manifest changed. Verification is the requested concept-file inspection, image dimensions and byte limits, and a folder-only diff. Runtime checks in `verify-game` are not evidence for unshipped concept paintings and were not run.
